## English

- The Space tab adds virtual 5.1/7.1 surround, head tracking, a virtual room, and 15 presets each for Speaker and Headphone.
- Each surround choice sounds different on both headphones and speakers.
- With AirPods, Roomcut uses your personalized spatial audio profile.
- The A/B Level button no longer flickers while it measures.
- The pkg and dmg include the license and third-party notices.
